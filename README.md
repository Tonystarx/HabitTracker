# Habit Tracker

A daily habit tracker built for iPhone, styled with Art Deco touches and the Stretch Timer color scheme.

## Features

- Unlimited habits, each on its own card with a checkmark for today and its current streak.
- Rest days: mark a day as rest and the streak carries on without counting that day.
- Streaks stay alive until midnight Eastern, so an unchecked today doesn't break them.
- Tap a habit to see its own calendar, edit past days, rename, archive, or delete it.
- Calendar tab with a combined month view (habits done out of habits due each day) and a week grid. Tap to edit any past day.
- Stats tab: today, perfect days, 7- and 30-day completion, best active and longest streaks, total check-ins, a 14-day chart, and a per-habit table.
- Drag to reorder habits. Archived habits keep their history and can be restored in Settings.
- Celebrations for streak milestones and perfect days.
- Works offline after the first visit, and installs to the Home Screen with its own icon.
- Data is saved on the device. Export/Import in Settings handles backups.

When you change index.html, bump `CACHE` in sw.js so installed copies pick up the update.
